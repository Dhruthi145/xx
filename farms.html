Farms

From the Soil Up. Sustainably.

Agriculture begins at the farm, and so do we. Viraj Holdings Farms practises integrated, sustainable farming across crop cultivation, horticulture, and livestock — building a self-sufficient foundation that powers every other business vertical in our ecosystem.

Our farms are living laboratories where traditional knowledge meets modern agronomy, producing high-quality raw material and demonstrating best practices to the farming communities we serve.

Who We Are

Integrated farming that feeds our ecosystem and fuels our purpose.

Viraj Holdings Farms is the agricultural backbone of the group — a vertically integrated farm operation spanning crop cultivation, seed production, horticulture, and livestock management.

We farm not just for produce but for purpose: to demonstrate sustainable practices, generate premium raw material for our processing units, and directly support the farming communities that our Agro Inputs business serves every day.

What We Do

Comprehensive Farm Operations

From seed to harvest and beyond, our farms cover every dimension of modern, sustainable agriculture.

🌾

Crop Cultivation

We cultivate a diverse range of food and cash crops — including paddy, maize, cotton, pulses, and oilseeds — using proven agronomic practices that maximise yield while protecting soil health.

🌱

Seed Production

Our farms serve as certified seed production plots in partnership with leading seed companies, maintaining strict quality standards to deliver high-germination seeds for the next season's planting.

🍅

Horticulture

We operate dedicated horticultural plots growing vegetables, fruits, and spices that supply fresh produce to local markets, processing units, and our FMCG portfolio.

🐄

Livestock & Dairy

Integrated livestock management adds a circular dimension to our farms — cattle provide organic manure that enriches our soils, while dairy output supports nutrition-focused FMCG products.

💧

Water Management

We invest in drip irrigation, rainwater harvesting, and watershed conservation to ensure efficient water use, reduce run-off, and safeguard the long-term productivity of our land.

🌿

Organic & Natural Farming

Dedicated organic farming blocks demonstrate chemical-free cultivation methods, producing certified-natural produce and serving as demonstration plots for farmers adopting sustainable practices.

Our Approach

Farming as a System, Not Just a Season

Integrated Farm Management

Our farms operate as closed-loop systems where crop residues feed livestock, animal waste enriches soil, and every resource is recycled within the farm boundary.

We layer technology — soil sensors, drone scouting, precision irrigation — on top of decades of agronomic wisdom to make data-driven decisions at every growth stage.

Core Philosophy

Healthy soil, responsible water use, and biodiversity are not costs — they are investments in the next generation of farming.

1

Plan

Soil testing, crop planning, and seasonal scheduling before every sowing cycle.

2

Cultivate

Precision inputs, mechanised operations, and continuous crop monitoring through the growing season.

3

Harvest & Channel

Timely harvest, quality grading, and seamless flow into our processing and trading verticals.

Why Our Farms

Why Viraj Holdings Farms Stand Apart

🔗

Vertically Integrated

Our farm produce flows directly into group processing, trading, and consumer businesses — eliminating middlemen and ensuring freshness, traceability, and better margins for every stakeholder.

🧪

Science-Backed Agronomy

Every crop variety, input, and irrigation schedule is backed by soil science, field trials, and agronomist recommendations — delivering consistently high-quality output season after season.

♻️

Circular & Sustainable

Waste from one operation becomes input for another. Crop residues, livestock manure, and organic matter are all recycled within our farm ecosystem, reducing external inputs and environmental impact.

📊

Demonstration & Training

Our farms act as demonstration plots where new technologies, varieties, and practices are trialled and showcased to partner farmers, encouraging adoption of better farming methods.

🤝

Community-Connected

By farming in the same regions where our dealers, distributors, and farmer customers operate, we build shared trust and create locally relevant, proven solutions.

Our Vision

A Model Farm Ecosystem for Modern India

We envision Viraj Holdings Farms as a nationally recognised model of integrated, sustainable agriculture — where cutting-edge technology, responsible stewardship, and commercial viability coexist to inspire the next generation of Indian farmers.

Our goal is to expand our farm footprint, deepen integration with every group vertical, and establish farmer training programmes that spread sustainable practices across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Karnataka.
